📖 Quick Guide

V-Die Width: Press brake ke neeche wala tool.
Default: 8 × Thickness

Bend Radius (R): Andar ki taraf ka curve.
Default: V / 6.5 ya R = T

K-Factor: Neutral axis position.
SS 304 = 0.44, SS 202 = 0.45

Springback: Material wapas aata hai.
SS ke liye 0.5° – 1.0° typical

Corner Relief: Corner ka cut size.
Default: R + T + 0.5mm
